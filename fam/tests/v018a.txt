# FM-FAM v0.18: the kick's sequence in two pages. Page 1: trig, pitch, volume, length. Page 2: condition, ratchet, (empty), nudge.

expect kpg 3
expect klen 16
# a new step: no shift, full volume
expect kpit2 0
expect kvol2 127
