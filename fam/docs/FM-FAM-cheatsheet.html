FM-FAM · Cheat sheet

FM-FAM at a glance

For FM-FAM 0.2.9 on the M-VAVE FM-1

DFAM style 16-step sequencer and a drum synth for the FM-1. It has two oscillators with 16 wave types, a kick with its own sequencer, parameter locks, a sound saved per pattern, an effects chain, random pages, scales, presets and projects. Bluetooth audio is experimental.

Install FM-FAM 0.2.9 Source code Print

FM-FAM is beta software. Use it at your own risk. It can have bugs and fail (wrong behaviour, lost data, an FM-1 that does not start until you install a firmware again), and installing it or restoring a backup writes to the FM-1's flash. Save what matters first and never unplug the USB cable while it writes. BUTTON a button, key or encoder of the FM-1  ·  tap, hold, turn  ·  A + B hold A while you use B  ·  K1-K4 are the four knobs, in the order of the screen footer  ·  the white keys are the steps

Getting around

SEQ EDIT LFO ARP FX GLO SCL SAVE ENV
Mode buttons: sequencer, synth, patch bay, kick, effects, patterns, scale, projects and presets, settings. The first tap goes to the mode, on the page you left; each further tap moves to the next page and wraps after the last. HOME does nothing alone.
SELECT turn
Change page inside the mode (does not wrap).
ALGORITHM turn
Move the cursor of the page: the step (SEQ pages 1-2, kick sequence), the target (patch bay), the bank or slot (patterns), the item (SAVE). Everywhere else it changes the tempo.
PRESETS turn
Next or previous preset of the mode (synth, kick, FX, patch bay, scale). In patterns it picks the pattern.
KNOB 1-KNOB 4
The four parameters named at the bottom of the screen: green, cyan, magenta and orange.
OCT- + KNOB
Coarse: 8 steps per click on 0-127 knobs, x4 on pitch, velocity, gate, kick pitch and volume, random and patch-bay amounts. Small selectors stay as they are. In SEQ and the kick's sequence pages it is the nudges.
PLAY
Start or stop. Hold 0.7 s to stop and go back to step 1.

Playing

STEP KEY tap
The 16 white keys are the steps in SEQ, SYNTH, patch bay and kick pages. Each tap cycles OFF, TRIG, ACCENT.
STEP KEY hold + KNOB
Edit that step. In SYNTH pages it sets a parameter lock for the step (the value turns yellow, LOCK). On kick pages it edits the kick sequence.
STEP KEY hold + REC
Clear the step (SEQ or kick sequence). A kick step also goes back to pitch 0 and volume 100%.
MIDI note in
By USB, or by Bluetooth with the BLUETOOTH page on. A note plays the synth; note 36 triggers the kick as it is, without step pitch or volume.

Sequencer

SEQ page 1
K1 pitch, K2 velocity, K3 gate (1-32 thirty-seconds of a step, or FULL), K4 ratchet (X1-X4).
SEQ page 2
K1 condition (1:1, 1:2, 2:2, 1:3 ... 4:4, RND), K2 trig, K3 pattern length (1-16), K4 swing.
SEQ page 3 TEMPO
K1 BPM ±1 (it accelerates), K2 BPM ±10. The BPM is shown large.
OCT- hold + KNOB 4
NUDGE: shifts the whole sequence one step per click, wrapping inside the pattern length. Trig, pitch, velocity, condition, gate, ratchet and locks travel with their steps; the kick sequence stays. The counter shows the shift since you pressed OCT-.
OCT- hold + KNOB 3
NUDGE ALL: the same, with the kick's sequence moving too (each inside its own length).

Kick

ARP page 1 KICK SEQUENCE 1
A sequence of its own, up to 16 steps, apart from the synth pattern. K1 trig (off, trig, accent), K2 pitch (-24 to +24 semitones), K3 volume (0-100%), K4 length (1-16). Step bars show the volume, accents in yellow.
ARP page 2 KICK SEQUENCE 2
K1 condition, K2 ratchet (X1-X4).
OCT- hold, on both pages
K3 NUDGE ALL (the kick's sequence and the synth's), K4 NUDGE KICK (the kick's alone).
ARP pages 3-5
3: pitch and decay. 4: tone (click, drive, level, wave). 5: FM and noise. 9 presets.
Which step
The one you hold with a white key or, if none, the one ALGORITHM points to.

Sound

EDIT (SYNTH), 6 pages
Two oscillators with 16 types each (TRI SAW SQR PLS SIN PWM SSAW FM ORGAN VOWEL BRASS FOLD DIGI METAL NOISE BELL); filter and noise (LP4, LP2, BP, HP); envelopes; mixer (levels, sync, volume); modulation (envelope to VCO 1, VCO 2 and filter, accent); output (attack, drive, glide, tune). 16 presets.
Switches (SYNC, SLOPE ...)
A switch changes at the first click: right is ON, left is OFF.
Choices (waves, filter type, delay time, slices)
Each click moves to the next choice.
LFO (patch bay)
Eight virtual cables: source to target, depth -100 to +100, connected or not. Step keys work here too. 7 presets.

Effects

FX, 7 pages
COMPRESSOR, DELAY (ping-pong), REVERB (with width), STUTTER, DJ FILTER (high-pass with resonance, 12 or 24 dB), LIMITER and FX MIXER. 10 presets.
Order
Compressor, stutter, delay, reverb, DJ filter, limiter.
KEY 1-KEY 6
White keys 1-6 on the effect pages: compressor, delay, reverb, stutter, DJ filter, limiter on or off.
FX MIXER
K1 delay mix, K2 reverb mix, K3 DJ filter cutoff, K4 stutter slice (1/8 to 1/128). White keys: 1 delay, 2 reverb, 3 DJ filter, 4 stutter, 5 compressor, 6 limiter. The last black key turns delay, reverb, DJ filter and stutter off together, and back on as they were.
Stutter key
Tap to latch it. Hold 350 ms or more to stutter only while it is pressed.

Random

REC
Opens the RANDOM page of the page you are on (23 pages); press again to go back. Each knob has an ON/OFF switch and an amount (0-100%).
What a page says
IN or NOT IN OCT+ + REC: whether RANDOM ALL draws this page. Under each bar, what that knob draws on a random of its page: a range (0-50), ANY for a choice, AUTO for a draw of its own.
RANDOM keys
White keys 1-4 switch a knob on or off, 5 all of them, 6 randomize this page, 7 RANDOM ALL.
OCT- + REC
Random of this page: whatever is switched on there (patch bay, FX and scale included). The reverb's width stays at 20 or under.
OCT+ + REC
RANDOM ALL: steps, synth sound and kick in one go. Cables, FX, scale and BPM stay.
OCT- + HOME
RANDOM ALL for melodies: a voice that holds a pitch (no noise, sync or cables), one of six scales with the quantizer on (the root stays), a phrase built from a four-step figure and a kick on every beat.
OCT+ + HOME
RANDOM ALL for electronic toms and low percussion: steps and kick as RANDOM ALL, and a synth that is a tom (a sine or triangle at a low pitch that falls, no attack, a decay that rings).
OCT+ + last white keys
On any page: the last white key mutes the kick, the one before it the synth; again, back on. A red S or K in the header says which is muted.
OCT- + GLO
INIT PAGE. SYNTH, kick sound and FX pages: the knobs go back to the first factory preset. Patch bay: every cable off. SEQ step pages: as in a new pattern, on every step, and no trigs. Kick line: empty. SCALE pages: as at first power-on.
OCT+ + GLO
INIT ALL: resets the current pattern, its steps and its sound. Tempo, other patterns, song and presets stay.
No holds
Every one of these acts at once, when the second button goes down.

Patterns & song

GLO page 1
Pattern grid: black keys are the banks A-K (11), white keys the patterns 1-16 of a bank (176 in all). While playing, the chosen pattern starts at the next bar. Actions: PLAY, COPY, PASTE, CLEAR, RAND.
GLO page 2 SONG
32 slots.
GLO pages 3-4 PATTERN SOUND
Every pattern keeps its own sound (synth, kick, FX, patch bay, scale) and loads it when it plays. Page 3 shows the presets it came from (EDITED if changed). Page 4: scale preset, TO BANK and TO ALL (copy this sound to the bank or to all 176 patterns), RESET.

Scale

SCL, 2 pages
Root, scale (16: chromatic, major, minor, dorian, phrygian, lydian, mixolydian, locrian, harmonic and melodic minor, pentatonic major and minor, blues, whole tone, diminished, hirajoshi), octave, quantize on/off; transpose, fine tune, humanize, spread. Any key sets the root. Everything but the kick is quantized. 13 presets.

Saving & loading

SAVE page 1 PROJECTS
4 slots (white keys 1-4). Pages 2-6: presets of SYNTH, KICK, FX, PATCH and SCALE (32 user slots each).
SAVE knobs
K1 item, K2 action (LOAD, SAVE, RENAME, CLEAR), K3 name cursor, K4 turn right to run it, or change the letter.
A project
Keeps the whole sound and banks A-C (48 patterns). Loading it replaces them.
Projects and backup
On the screen mirror page (Chrome or Edge, USB): Read the FM-1, then save a project to a file or replace one with a file, or save and load everything (sound, patterns, presets, projects). Stop the sequencer first; the FM-1 asks SEL to confirm, HOME to cancel.
Autosave
There is no save key. 1.5 s after your last change, with the sequencer stopped and nothing sounding; only what changed. While playing nothing is written (a flash write silences the audio for a moment). The SAVE action of a project or preset writes at once.

Settings (ENV), 5 pages

Page 1 AUDIO
K1 USB audio ON/OFF (off: the computer records silence). K2 speaker + jack ON/OFF: one output, a plug switches the speaker off. K3 USB level: MASTER's, or FULL. Kept when you switch off: no sound? look here.
Page 2 MIDI: clock and sync
K1 CLOCK IN: the MIDI clock from USB, the MIDI jack or Bluetooth plays the steps; its start and stop run the sequencer (header: EXT). K2 CLOCK OUT: clock, start, stop to USB and Bluetooth. K3 PULSE OUT, for gear with a sync input: 1, 2, 4 or 24 a quarter note; on the jack, pulses left (tip), sound right (ring).
Page 3 MIDI: channels
K1 IN: ALL or 1-16 (note 36 is the kick). K2 OUT: OFF or 1-16, the synth's steps as notes. K3 KICK: OFF or 1-16, its steps as note 36. Out to USB and Bluetooth.
Pages 4 and 5
BLUETOOTH (below) and VERSION: the firmware's version (0.2.9) and the CPU load. The MIDI jack is an input only and there is no pulse input: the FM-1 follows MIDI clock, not pulses.

Bluetooth (experimental)

ENV page 4
The BLUETOOTH page. K1 ON/OFF (right is ON). K2, ALGORITHM or PRESETS scroll the devices (KEPT are remembered, NEW were found). K3 picks the action: SCAN, CONNECT, HANG UP, FORGET, OUTPUT, QUALITY, BT MIDI. K4 turned right runs it.
Pair headphones
Put them in pairing mode, K1 ON, SCAN, pick one with K2, CONNECT. Next time, CONNECT on the KEPT device (four are remembered).
OUTPUT
While linked, the sound goes to Bluetooth and the cable output is muted. OUTPUT switches it back.
QUALITY
HIGH, MID or LOW with each turn of K4. A read-out shows slots, bitpool and drops.
BT MIDI
Switches the Bluetooth MIDI announcement on or off (on by default). Notes sound as they do by USB.
Limits
The sound arrives 150-300 ms late: for listening, not for playing live. A device that accepts only one-slot packets sounds worse. After changing firmware, forget the FM-1 on the phone or speaker and pair again. Bluetooth is off at power-on unless you left it on.

Good to know

Random character
Each draw picks one: mostly low sounds, 15% of the time bright hi-hat sounds. Pitched steps use a minor pentatonic.
Screen mirror
Mirrors the screen and panel over USB (Chrome or Edge). The same page saves projects and backups, and installs versions.
Leave FM-FAM
Install any other firmware package with The Firmware Hub or M-UPGRADE. The package identity is FM-1_111.
